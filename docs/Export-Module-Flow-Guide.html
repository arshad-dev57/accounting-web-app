Export Module Flow Guide

Company presentation / self-study notes — how a real export deal works and how it maps to the ERP module.

1. Big Picture (Simple)

Your company sells goods from Pakistan to an overseas buyer (UAE, Europe, etc.). The full job has 3 phases:

1. Make the deal
Inquiry → Quote → Order → Proforma
2. Ship the goods
Pack → Customs → Shipment → Docs → Dispatch
3. Collect money
Advance / LC → Settlement receipt → Expenses → Complete

2. One-Slide Flow

Inquiry → Quotation → Export Order → Proforma ↓ Packing → Customs (LEO) → Shipment → Documents ↓ Dispatch (Invoice + Stock + AR) → Receipts / LC → Expenses → Completed

3. Phase 1 — Make the Deal

Step 1 — Export Inquiry

Real life
Buyer from Dubai messages: “We need 1200 meters fabric, CIF Jebel Ali rate please.”

Module screen
Export Inquiries — log buyer, product, qty, target price, port, Incoterm.

“First step is lead capture. Every overseas request is recorded so the pipeline is not lost.”

Step 2 — Export Quotation

Real life
You finalize price and send a formal offer (FOB/CIF, freight, validity).

Module screen
Export Quotations — generate from inquiry → Approve / Accept.

“Inquiry is interest only. Quotation is the formal offer. Approve before converting to order.”

Step 3 — Export Sales Order

Real life
Buyer says “OK, book the order.”

Module screen
On quotation click Convert to Export Order → number like ESO-... (Confirmed).

“From here the commitment starts — packing, shipping and finance planning begin.”

Step 4 — Proforma Invoice

Real life
Buyer needs a PI to send advance TT or to open a Letter of Credit at the bank.

Module screen
Proforma Invoices

“Commercial invoice comes later when goods leave. PI comes first for payment arrangement.”

4. Phase 2 — Ship the Goods

Step 5 — Packing & Inspection

Real life
Goods packed in container, sealed; sometimes SGS / third-party inspection.

Module screen
Packing & Inspection → Sample Tested → Passed & Certified (enter certificate number before certifying).

Step 6 — Customs & Declaration (Pakistan)

Real life
Shipping bill filed, customs inspects, then issues LEO (Let Export Order).

Module screen
Customs & Declaration:
Draft → Submitted to Customs → Goods Inspected at Port → LEO Granted

“Without LEO the container cannot legally sail. This is Pakistan export clearance.”

Step 7 — Shipments & Tracking

Real life
Book with shipping line (Maersk, etc.): vessel, B/L, Karachi → Jebel Ali.

Module screen
Shipments & Tracking — create booking first; later move milestones (Gate-In → Loaded → In Transit → Arrived → Customs Cleared Buyer).

Step 8 — Export Documents

Real life
Set of papers for bank / buyer:

  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Bill of Lading
  • Certificate of Origin
  • Insurance Certificate (for CIF)

Module screen
Export Documents — create each doc and move status (e.g. Finalized).

Step 9 — Export Dispatch (most important accounting step)

Real life
Warehouse gate-out → truck to port → gate-in → handover to shipping line.

Module screen
Export Dispatch → En Route → Gate-In Complete → Handed Over to Shipping Line

At handover the system automatically:

  • Posts the commercial invoice
  • Deducts stock
  • Creates Accounts Receivable
“Earlier steps are planning/ops. Dispatch is where ERP books are updated.”

5. Phase 3 — Collect Money

Step 10 — Receipts & Settlement

Real life
Buyer pays by TT, or LC is negotiated and bank credits your account.

Module screen
Receipts & Settlement
Optional Advance against PI → Settlement against commercial invoice → Realized & Credited (post).

Enter foreign amount in USD (e.g. full order total if no advance).

Step 11 — Finance & LC Tracking (if LC deal)

Real life
Bank advises LC → confirms → negotiates documents → pays.

Module screen
Finance & LC Tracking

“Cash/TT deals can live on Receipts alone. LC deals need this bank lifecycle tracker.”

Step 12 — Expenses & Duties

Real life
Ocean freight, inspection, bank charges, duties, haulage.

Module screen
Expenses & Duties → create draft → Post to ledger.

Step 13 — Close the deal

  • Shipment → Customs Cleared Buyer
  • Customs → Sailed & Completed
  • Order → Completed

6. Why this module helps the company

Real export language
Inquiry, PI, LEO, BL, LC — terms exporters already know.
Ops linked to accounts
Dispatch posts invoice, stock and AR automatically.
FX aware
USD contract with PKR books and exchange rates.
Visibility
Dashboard/reports by country, outstanding and receipts.

7. 2-Minute Demo Script

“Buyer in Dubai sends an inquiry. We approve a quotation and convert it to an export order, then issue a proforma so payment can be arranged. After packing and customs LEO, we book the shipment and prepare documents. When dispatch is handed to the shipping line, the system posts the invoice and stock. Foreign remittance is then settled, and the order is completed.”

8. Practice Checklist

  • ☐ Create Inquiry (buyer + product + qty + price + port)
  • ☐ Generate Quotation from inquiry → Approve
  • ☐ Convert to Export Order
  • ☐ Create Proforma
  • ☐ Packing → certify (with certificate no.)
  • ☐ Customs → LEO Granted
  • ☐ Create Shipment
  • ☐ Add key Documents
  • ☐ Dispatch → Handed Over
  • ☐ Settlement Receipt → Realized & Credited
  • ☐ Advance shipment statuses → Complete order
